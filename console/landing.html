How it works

From order to delivery in 4 steps

Designed for teams with real delivery SLAs — inspired by how Flexport and Locus run their operations.

01
Add inventory
Create products, warehouses, and add stock. Import from CSV or API. Low-stock alerts fire automatically.
02
Receive orders
Orders arrive via console, CSV, or API. Each gets a customer-facing tracking link automatically.
03
Auto-plan dispatch
Click "Auto Plan" — Prodis groups orders by area, assigns drivers, and creates optimized routes.
04
Track & complete
Watch drivers on the live map. Stock auto-deducts on dispatch. Analytics update in real-time.
